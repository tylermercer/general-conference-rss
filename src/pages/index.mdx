# General Conference RSS Feed

A reliable, drip-fed RSS feed of General Conference talk links.

## Why this feed exists

This project automatically scrapes the official conference talk links and provides a modern RSS feed for your favorite feed reader.

## How drip feeding works

Talks are evenly paced across the calendar gap between conferences in broadcast order. Instead of receiving 30+ talks at once on conference weekend, you receive a fresh talk every few days as you prepare for the next conference.

## Current status

The feed provides direct links and titles to conference talks (English, 2026 onwards). Audio enclosures are currently stubbed and will be added in a future update.

## Subscribe

Copy and paste the site URL directly into your RSS reader (autodiscovery will find the feed), or use the direct feed link:
